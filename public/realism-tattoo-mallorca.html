What realism demands

Realism is the hardest
discipline in tattooing.
It has no place to hide.
Every millimetre shows.

A realism tattoo is not a filtered photo applied to skin. It is a construction — light, shadow, texture, contrast — built specifically for the surface and placement of a human body. Done wrong, it fades into grey within five years. Done right, it is among the most striking things a person can carry. At Blackthorn, Tony approaches every realism piece as a standalone composition, not a copy of a reference image.

What we tattoo

Portrait realism

People, faces, expressions. The most technically demanding category in realism tattooing. We do not take portrait commissions lightly. The consultation process for portraits is longer — Tony needs to understand not just the image but the story behind it. The work reflects both.

Animal & wildlife

Texture, fur, feather, scale. Animals reward the kind of patient, layered approach that realism requires. Dogs, cats, birds, horses — each species has its own technical challenges. Tony has developed specific techniques for each type of animal texture.

Botanical & nature

Flowers, plants, natural elements rendered with photographic precision. A different kind of realism — more about organic structure than mimicking photography. Often combined with fine line elements for a piece that bridges both disciplines.

How realism works at Blackthorn

Realism requires more preparation than any other tattoo style. The process below is not optional — it is what separates work that lasts from work that does not.

01

Reference & discussion

You share your references, your placement, and your vision. Tony asks questions — the consultation is a creative dialogue, not a form to fill in.

02

Custom drawing

The piece is drawn from scratch, adapted for your specific body placement. Not a print of a reference — a tattoo design.

03

Design approval

You see the design before the session. Revisions are discussed. Nothing is applied to your skin until you are certain.

04

Session in Campos

The session takes as long as it takes. No rushing. Natural light, clean studio, Tony's full focus on one piece, one client, one sitting.

Before you book

How much does a realism tattoo cost in Mallorca?

Realism is priced by session time and complexity, not by size alone. A standard realism piece at Blackthorn runs between €250 and €500. Large-scale or multi-session realism projects are quoted individually after the consultation. Contact us with your project and we will give you a specific figure.

Do you do portrait tattoos in Mallorca?

Yes. Portrait realism is one of Tony's specialisations. It requires more consultation time than other styles — the design process for portraits is longer and more detailed. If you are considering a portrait, contact us early. We do not take portrait sessions without a proper consultation first.

Can I book a realism session while visiting Mallorca?

Yes. Realism sessions for visiting clients require planning — the design process happens remotely before your arrival. When you come to the studio, the session is focused entirely on execution. Allow adequate time in your schedule: realism sessions are not short, and we do not rush them.

How do I send my reference images?

Through the booking form on our main site. High-resolution images help. Include notes on what you want to keep, what you want to adapt, and where on your body you are considering placement. Tony reviews everything before responding.
