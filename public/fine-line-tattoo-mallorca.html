The technique

What fine line actually is

Single-needle or tight-grouped needle work that creates hair-thin lines, precise shading, and intricate detail without heavy black fill. The result is graphic, precise, and when done correctly, permanent. It requires a very controlled hand, quality pigment, and an understanding of how individual skin holds ink.

What makes it fail

Speed. Wrong needle depth. Cheap pigment. Artists who dabble in fine line rather than specialise in it. A fine line tattoo that blurs in three years was never fine line — it was marketing. We have built the entire studio around doing this right, every single time.

Before you book

How much does a fine line tattoo cost in Mallorca?

Pricing at Blackthorn starts at €150 for small pieces and scales with size, complexity, and session time. A standard fine line session runs €200–350. Large or multi-session projects are quoted individually. We do not post a price list — every piece is different. Contact us for a specific quote.

How far in advance should I book if I'm visiting Mallorca?

For high season (June–September), we recommend booking 3–4 weeks in advance. Slots fill quickly during summer. If you are planning a trip to Mallorca and want to include a session at Blackthorn, book before you book your flights.

Do you do walk-ins?

No. Every session at Blackthorn is booked in advance. Fine line work requires preparation — design, stencil, placement discussion. Walk-in work and fine line are not compatible. If you want quality, you plan ahead.

Can I book a session while on holiday in Mallorca?

Yes. Many of our clients are visiting the island. We handle remote consultation before you arrive — design is approved in advance so the session itself is focused on execution. You leave Mallorca with the piece done. Allow at least 48 hours between the session and your flight for the initial healing stage.

Where exactly are you located?

Carrer de Santanyí 19, Campos, Mallorca. Campos is 40 minutes south of Palma, 10 minutes from Es Trenc beach. Free street parking. If you are staying in Santanyí, Ses Salines, Colònia de Sant Jordi, or the south coast, we are your closest serious studio.
