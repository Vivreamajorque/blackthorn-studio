Before you book

Is there a good tattoo studio near Es Trenc / Santanyí / Cala d'Or?

Blackthorn Tattoo in Campos is the closest serious studio to all of south Mallorca's premium coast. Santanyí is 15 minutes away. Ses Salines 12, Cala d'Or 20, Porto Petro 22. You do not need to drive to Palma for quality work.

Can I book a tattoo in south Mallorca while on holiday?

Yes. We work regularly with visitors to the island. Remote consultation before your trip means the design is ready when you arrive. Allow 48 hours between your session and your return flight for the initial healing phase.

How do I book a session at Blackthorn?

Use the booking form on our main site. Describe your project, include references if you have them, and indicate your preferred dates and session timing. Tony responds within 24 hours. A deposit confirms your slot.

How far is Blackthorn from the airport?

Approximately 35 minutes from Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI). If you are flying in specifically for a session, we can accommodate morning bookings. Combine with a stay in the south of the island — Campos, Santanyí, or Ses Salines are all within 15 minutes.
